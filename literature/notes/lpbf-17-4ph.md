## Starting points (verify against sources and cite)

- **Retained austenite in as-built parts** is affected by:
  - nitrogen from N₂-atomised powder or N₂ shielding gas (stabilises austenite);
  - fast cooling (10⁵–10⁶ K/s) and a fine microstructure.
  - Ar-atomised powder tends to give mostly martensite or δ-ferrite instead.
  - Key papers: Murr 2012, Pasebani 2018, Vunnam 2019, Facchini 2010.
- **Effects of austenite:** it can transform under strain (TRIP effect), giving lower yield strength with strong work hardening.
- **Ageing directly after printing works poorly** on austenite-rich parts; solution treatment first (± cryogenic treatment) helps (LeBrun 2015, Sun 2018).
- **Intrinsic heat treatment:** each new layer reheats the layers below it.
- **Defects and anisotropy:**
  - porosity from lack of fusion or keyholing;
  - columnar grains along the build direction;
  - residual stress.
- **Link to the paper:** "same laser settings, different phase" is a hidden variable — exactly the kind of scatter Wong et al. discuss.
